Games & User Experience

Welcome here! My name is Vincent. I make games and improve apps and services. Having worked on complex strategy games for the past three years, I gathered a lot of experience in game design, programming, and user experience design. It has always been a top priority for me to ensure interaction flows and game features are intuitive and satisfying for players, and I am convinced that proper focus on the user experience is what sets an awesome thing apart from one that just works.

Design, games, and technology have been lifelong passions for me. One of the influences this started with was collecting, and later crafting my own trading cards in elementary school. This website's playing card theming is in reference to that. Pick a card below to read more.
